Pause Blocking

Partial pause allows all sites except your really bad sites. Complete pause allows every site. Normal blocking resumes when the timer runs out.

Really Bad Sites

These domains and their subdomains stay blocked during a partial pause, even if allowlisted. Complete pause allows them. Outside a pause, they follow the normal blocklist and allowlist rules.

    Blocklist
